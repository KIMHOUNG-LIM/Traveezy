WHO WE ARE

Connecting You to the Heart of Cambodia

Traveezy is a modern Cambodian travel platform created to simplify how you explore our country's rich history, stunning natural landscapes, and vibrant food culture.

Whether you are a local seeking a refreshing weekend getaway in Kampot, or an international adventurer discovering the magnificent temples of Angkor for the first time, Traveezy provides curated guides, verified hotels, and local insight every step of the way.

  • Handpicked destinations & hidden local gems
  • Verified hotel listings & authentic restaurant guides
  • Built with pride by local Cambodian developers

Our Mission

To make travel across Cambodia accessible, stress-free, and deeply enjoyable for everyone. We aim to highlight local communities, preserve cultural heritage, and empower travelers with reliable, seamless tools to plan their dream trips.

Our Vision

To become Cambodia’s leading digital travel ecosystem, celebrated globally for inspiring sustainable tourism, supporting local enterprise, and showcasing Cambodia as one of the world's most welcoming destinations.
