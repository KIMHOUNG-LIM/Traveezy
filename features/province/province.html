Siem Reap at a Glance

Essential geography, heritage milestones, and travel insights to plan your dream journey with confidence.

UNESCO World Heritage

Angkor Archaeological Park covers 400+ km² of sacred land with over 1,000 recorded monuments, preserved as a global treasure since 1992.

Best Travel Weather

Nov to Feb: Pleasant cool breezes (24°C–30°C). Jun to Oct: Emerald green season with lush moats and fewer crowds.

Currency & Payments

US Dollars ($) and Cambodian Riel (KHR ~4,000 KHR = $1) are dual currencies. Digital Bakong KHQR is accepted everywhere.

Modern SAI Airport

Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport (SAI) opened in 2023, offering world-class facilities and seamless express transit to town (45 mins).

Iconic Local Remork

Travel like a local with traditional open-air Cambodian Tuk-Tuks (Remork), or book instantly via Grab & PassApp ride-hailing.

Legendary Hospitality

Renowned as one of Southeast Asia's safest and friendliest destinations for solo adventurers, honeymooners, and family vacations.

Siem Reap Travel Tips & Insider Guide

Practical, tested advice for both local Cambodians and international travelers to ensure a smooth, rewarding journey.

Angkor Pass Guide

Essential

• 1-Day Pass: $37 USD
• 3-Day Pass: $62 USD (valid 10 days)
• 7-Day Pass: $72 USD (valid 1 month)
Cambodian citizens enter free with national ID.

Pro-Tip: Passes bought after 5:00 PM grant free entry to watch sunset that same evening!

Temple Dress Code

Required

Angkor is an active sacred sanctuary. Both men and women must cover shoulders and knees. Sleeveless shirts and tight wraps may be denied entry at upper temple levels.

Etiquette: Remove hats inside active shrines and ask politely before photographing monks.

Sunrise & Sunset

Photo Spots

• Sunrise: Arrive by 5:00 AM at the Angkor Wat Left Pond for mirror reflection shots.
• Sunset: Pre Rup, Phnom Bakheng (arrive early; max 300 capacity), or Srah Srang.

Bring: A small flashlight or phone light for walking pre-dawn paths.

Getting Around

Tuk-Tuk & Apps

Use Grab or PassApp for transparent, metered town rides ($1–$3). For full-day temple tours, hire a dedicated Remork driver ($18–$25/day) who provides cold water and shade.

Alternative: Rent an eco-friendly e-bike or bicycle to explore shaded forest trails at your own pace.

Money & Digital Pay

KHQR & Cash

Keep crisp USD bills or Cambodian Riel (KHR ~4,000 = $1). Today, the Bakong Tourist App and KHQR QR codes allow instant cashless payments at almost all cafes, shops, and stalls.

ATMs: Readily available in town dispensing both USD and KHR.

Responsible Travel

Eco Friendly

Support ethical social enterprises (Phare Circus, Artisans Angkor, Apopo HeroRATs). Carry a reusable flask to use the free Refill Not Landfill water stations across town.

Child Protection: Please do not give money to child vendors so they stay in school.

We recommend at least 3 to 4 full days. This allows 2 days for the Angkor temples (Small and Grand Circuits), 1 day for Tonle Sap floating villages and Banteay Srei / Phnom Kulen waterfalls, and evening time to enjoy Phare Circus, night markets, and local cuisine.

The new Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport (SAI) is located 45 km east of town. You can take an official airport taxi or private van (~$35–$40), airport shuttle bus (~$8), or pre-arrange airport pick-up with your hotel. Travel time is approximately 45 to 55 minutes via the direct expressway.

Yes! You can purchase official e-passes securely on the Angkor Enterprise official portal with Visa/Mastercard, or in person at the official ticket center. Credit cards, debit cards, USD, and KHR cash are all accepted.
