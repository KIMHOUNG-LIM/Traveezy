DISCOVER SIEM REAP

Why Visit Siem Reap?

Explore ancient wonders, vibrant local culture, traditional floating villages, and breathtaking nature.

Ancient Wonder of Angkor

Home to the world's largest religious monument, Angkor Wat, and the mysterious stone carved faces of Bayon. Walking through these ancient Khmer temples feels like stepping directly back into majestic history.

Rich Cultural Heritage

Beyond the ruins, immerse yourself in living Khmer culture. Witness mesmerizing Apsara dance performances, discover traditional silk weaving, and explore vibrant night markets filled with hand-made crafts.

Village Life in Preah Dak

Escape the busy city and experience genuine Cambodian warmth in Preah Dak Village. Watch local artisans craft fresh palm sugar, admire classic wooden stilt houses, and taste authentic Nom Banh Chok noodles.

Majestic Floating Villages

Discover a unique aquatic lifestyle on Tonle Sap Lake. Take a wooden boat cruise through floating communities, schools, and lush mangrove forests that shift dynamically across the seasons.

TOP ACTIVITIES

Travel Around Amazing Siem Reap

Sunrise at Angkor Wat & Ta Prohm

Watch iconic towers silhouette against the morning golden sky, then explore jungle-entwined temple ruins.

Jungle Trekking & Waterfalls

Hike through Phnom Kulen National Park to discover sacred river carvings and refreshing natural waterfalls.

Vibrant Pub Street Nightlife

Experience lively night markets, street food stalls, live music, and energetic evening entertainment.

Boat Cruise on Tonle Sap Lake

Sail past stilt houses and floating mangrove forests on Southeast Asia's largest freshwater lake.
