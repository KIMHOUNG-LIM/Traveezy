TRAVEL WISDOM

How to Experience Siem Reap Like a Local

Essential tips for transparent transit, temple etiquette, and memorable local interactions.

Tuk-Tuks & Remorks

Ride-Hailing Apps

Download Grab or PassApp on your smartphone for transparent, fixed-price rides across town ($1–$3). For distant excursions (Banteay Srei or Kulen), hire a dedicated Remork driver ($20–$35/day) who will wait for you with chilled water bottles.

Respect & Etiquette

Sacred Conduct

Angkor and pagodas are living spiritual centers. Keep shoulders and knees covered with breathable linen. Greet locals with the traditional Sampeah (pressed palms), and avoid touching anyone on the head or pointing your feet at Buddha statues.

Cash & Digital KHQR

Seamless Payments

Cash is accepted in both US Dollars ($) and Cambodian Riel (KHR ~4,000 = $1). The Bakong Tourist KHQR system allows contactless payments at local street stalls, cafes, and markets without needing heavy currency notes.
